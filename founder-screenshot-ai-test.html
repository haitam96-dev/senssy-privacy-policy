Senssy — Founder-Only Screenshot AI Testing Notice

Scope: This notice applies only to controlled, internal testing of Senssy's screenshot-recognition feature by the developer. The feature is not available to external beta testers or the public.

Screenshot processing

With your explicit permission, Senssy processes a screenshot you select and transmits a resized copy through its Supabase backend to OpenAI for AI analysis. The purpose is to identify the product name, displayed price, currency when reliably identifiable, and the location of the product photograph. Results can be reviewed and corrected.

Privacy considerations

Screenshots may contain information beyond the product. Do not upload account details, sensitive personal information, or information belonging to other people during this test.

Storage

The screenshot and cropped product photograph are retained locally for the product workflow. Supabase maintains consent records and request metadata for security and usage limits. OpenAI states that API data is not used for model training by default and may retain information for abuse monitoring, generally for up to 30 days, subject to exceptions.

Your choices

Screenshot AI recognition is optional and separate from AI Companion consent. You can decline and enter product details manually. During this development test, permission can be checked, granted, or withdrawn using the temporary Screen 43 developer controls. Withdrawal blocks future analysis but does not erase information previously processed.
